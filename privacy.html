Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version: this app doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you, has no accounts and no ads. Everything it does happens on your own phone.

What is saved on your device

To remember your choices and work offline, the app saves these in your browser's storage on your device only:

  • your campus, programme code, level and group
  • your appearance choice (light, dark or auto)
  • your calendar alert time and the date classes end
  • a timetable you upload yourself, if you do
  • whether you've seen the privacy notice
  • a copy of the app's files, so it opens without internet

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else. You can delete it all below.

Files you upload or create

When you upload a timetable, the Excel file is read on your phone and is not uploaded anywhere. PDFs and calendar files are made on your phone too. When you share one, it goes only to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or email.

Hosting

The website is served by a hosting company. Like most websites, its servers may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and the time of a visit, for security and to keep the site running. The developer doesn't use these logs to identify anyone. See your hosting provider's privacy policy for details.

Your data, your choice

This removes your saved choices, any uploaded timetable and the offline copy. Events already added to your calendar stay there; delete those in your calendar app.

Changes and contact

If the app ever starts collecting information (for example, visitor statistics), this page will be updated first and you will be asked before anything is turned on.

The app is built and run by Callistus Aidoo. Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer.
